# Fieldcraft v10: model and image credits

## Imported models

**Powered by Poly Haven.** These 25 added model families were downloaded from the official Poly Haven API and asset files. All are offered by their creators under CC0 1.0. Source/licence: https://polyhaven.com/license . API documentation: https://github.com/Poly-Haven/Public-API . This game ships the assets locally and does not rely on the API at runtime.

Texture maps are resized to at most 384 pixels. Unused animation tracks and unused modules from multi-part architecture kits are removed. Files are packaged as self-contained GLBs. Templates reuse geometry/materials for placement and item inspection. Some geometry is fitted to existing gameplay footprints to retain collision behaviour. Original author metadata is also in `assets/v10/model-manifest.json`.

## Item visuals and thumbnails

All 61 catalogue entries have a real model that can be rendered and inspected. Twenty-five item definitions reference imported meshes, including the earlier licensed equipment collection. Thirty-six use original Field Signal geometry for game-specific, schematic or unavailable objects. Exact per-item mapping: `assets/v10/item-visuals.json`. The item inspector labels imported versus authored geometry.

The inventory images are snapshots of these same models, rendered by the game's own shared studio. They are not stock photographs, not AI-generated substitutes for absent models, and not a WebGL context per item. The original author/licence still applies to imported models shown in screenshots.

Some catalogue labels use representative shapes. The service-pistol mesh is not claimed to be an exact PM replica; food, ammunition, clothing and document visuals may reuse representative assets. Remaining exact item-specific refinements are explicitly on the roadmap.

Original authored additions include the atlas UI, model-viewer code, map gestures, game-specific item meshes and catalogue. The existing Third-party licences for Three.js, Pyodide, previous models and music remain in V7_CREDITS.md, V8_CREDITS.md, V9_CREDITS.md and the individual asset records.

No font files are redistributed. No paid asset licences, attribution waivers or third-party endorsements are claimed.
