## Radio and lighting audio

- **The Accordion Sample IV**, by **Spring Spring**, obtained from OpenGameArt. The contributor offers CC0 and OGA-BY 3.0; this release uses the CC0 option. https://opengameart.org/content/the-accordion-sample-iv
  The recording is high/low-pass filtered, loudness-normalised, converted to a mono MP3, and loop-spliced after decoding. It is a stylised folk/chiptune accordion broadcast, not an authentic Ukrainian or Russian radio recording and not a claim of a particular national traditional composition.
- Fluorescent ballast hum, electrical ticks and soft lamp noise are original procedural sound design. Visual flicker and audio modulation consume the same fixture-state signal, with a short click-prevention audio ramp. Physical display/audio latency still depends on the device.
- Previous soundtrack, drone, weather and gameplay recordings are retained. The source backup preserves the unbundled recordings and their licences. Runtime uses an audio bank solely to reduce deployment file count.
