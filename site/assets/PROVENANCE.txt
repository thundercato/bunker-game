FIELD SIGNAL: FIRST LIGHT / ASSET PROVENANCE

Scene geometry for the trees, refuge, hut, handheld props, firearm and Watcher is original. The two roadside barriers use a reduced CC0 scan, credited below. No assets from Into the Radius or STALKER are included.

The wood and rust materials are original procedural artwork. The fern cutout, start artwork, backpack icon and application icons are reused from the user's own Field Signal v3 project.

The 24 bundled MP3 source files are unchanged from Field Signal v3 recovery. Original procedural music and sound design, without third-party audio samples. Audio loop boundaries are repaired non-destructively after decoding. The stream is synthesised, not a field recording.

Three.js r170: MIT, https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r170/LICENSE

Additional assets:
